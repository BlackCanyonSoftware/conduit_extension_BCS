Rules

A rule says what Conduit may capture, from which site, and where it goes. Nothing is captured until you create a rule and approve it, and you can switch a rule off or pause everything at any time.

Rules you import start switched off and need your approval, just like rules you make here.

Local apps

An app is a program that receives values from Conduit. Add one by its address, or let Conduit look on this computer. The app tells Conduit what it would like and why; you choose which requests to approve, and each becomes a rule you can switch off.

Apps on this computer need nothing extra. An app on another computer must use https and you confirm it by typing its name, and it stays marked as not on this computer.

Delivery log

Every send to an app is listed here with its time, rule, size and result. The log never holds the values that were sent. A failed delivery is shown straight away and tried again a few times; pausing capture also pauses delivery.

Capture log

Each time an approved rule captures a value it is saved here, in this browser only. The log keeps the newest 1,000 entries. Values are hidden until you choose Show.

The downloaded log contains the values: keep it as private as a password.
